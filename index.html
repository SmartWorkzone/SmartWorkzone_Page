The Smart Work Zone System is an integrated safety and monitoring platform designed to address work zone intrusions by improving situational awareness, worker protection, and traffic management in highway work zones. The system combines sensing technologies, real-time risk prediction, digital twin, and adaptive feedback mechanisms to support safer interactions among workers, vehicles, and work zone environments. The system continuously monitors both traffic conditions and worker activities within the work zone. By combining data from multiple sensing devices, the platform can identify potential safety risks and provide timely alerts to workers, drivers, and traffic management personnel. The Smart Work Zone System can be used by transportation agencies, construction contractors, traffic control personnel, researchers, and work zone safety managers. The platform is designed for both stationary and mobile work zone environments and can support field operations, safety monitoring, and research activities.

This project is supported by the Indiana Department of Transportation (INDOT) (#SPR-4835) and the United States Department of Transportation (USDOT) SMART Grant Program (#69A3552541003). The system was developed through collaborations with industry partners (PSS Innovation, Ramudden, Butterfly Junction Technologies, Indiana Sign and Barricade, Aramsco) to support practical deployment, field implementation, and real-world work zone operations.
